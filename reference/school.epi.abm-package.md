# school.epi.abm: A Model of Infectious Disease Epidemic and Mitigation Dynamics in K-6 Schools

Implements an agent-based model of infectious disease epidemic and
mitigation dynamics in K-6 schools, as described in Morrison et al.
(2023)
[doi:10.3389/fpubh.2023.856940](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2023.856940)
.
